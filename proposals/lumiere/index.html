About us
19231923

The Ariss family has been in the lighting business since 1923. The third generation founded Lumière Group in Beirut in 1994, starting with a handful of people.

1923

The family opens its first lighting business.

1994

Lumière Group is founded in Beirut.

Today

Over 150 employees in Lebanon, Saudi Arabia and Jordan, with offices in Qatar, Kuwait and the UAE.

Services

One team for the whole project.

Most of our clients come to us with drawings and leave with a building that is lit, focused and handed over. Some only need one part of that, which is fine too.

Lighting design

Concepts, lux calculations, fixture schedules and control strategies, presented with renders before anything is ordered.

Technical consultancy

Specification reviews, glare and energy studies, and on-site mock-ups for client approval.

Project management

Coordination with contractors, delivery schedules, installation supervision, commissioning and focusing.

Supply and after-sales

European brands supplied from stock or to order, with local warranty and service.
